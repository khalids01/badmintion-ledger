Redesigned for simplicity. Four tabs — Home, Members, Transactions, Reports — with Settings in the header. Home is a clear cash hero plus dues and spending, two quick actions (collect money, add expense), the members who still owe, and recent transactions. Members is a compact searchable list opening a detail screen with fees, payments, dues, loans and history, and a preselected Record-payment action. Transactions are date-grouped with search and advanced filters hidden until needed. Reports holds all charts, date range and CSV exports. Dues live under Members → Dues. One global Add button opens a sheet: Collect money, Add expense, Borrow or repay, Assign dues. Advanced fields are collapsed under "Advanced details". The redesigned layout works for approved read-only members too, without admin actions. Payment-to-loan-repayment stays: one save, full fee credit, both entries linked. Includes full English and Bangla labels.
